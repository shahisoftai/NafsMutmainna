# Pathway 5 — Sin → Love

> The chain from the guilt of sin to the warmth of love for Allah. A pathway for the user who has stumbled and wants to come back.

---

## 1 · Summary

```
   (Tawbah)  →  Inabah  →  Raja  →  Mahabbah
   (Repentance)   (Turning)   (Hope)   (Love)
```

| Step | Attribute | Nature | One-line definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Tawbah** | Positive | Sincere repentance from sin, with the resolve not to return. |
| 2 | **Inabah** | Positive | The conscious *turning* toward Allah after Tawbah. |
| 3 | **Raja** | Positive | Hope in Allah's mercy — the fruit of true Tawbah. |
| 4 | **Mahabbah** | Positive | Love of Allah — the highest station of the heart. |

## 3 · Recommended practices

### 3.1 Dhikr (daily)

- *Astaghfirullah* — 100×/day. (For Tawbah.)
- *Allahumma inni anabtu ilayka* — 7× in sujood. (For Inabah.)
- *Ya Rahman, ya Rahim* — 100×. (For Raja.)
- *Allahumma inni as'aluka hubbaka wa hubba man yuhibbuka* — daily. (For Mahabbah.)

### 3.2 Dua (memorise)

1. *"Allahumma anta Rabbi la ilaha illa anta, khalaqtani wa ana 'abduka, wa ana 'ala 'ahdika wa wa'dika mastata'tu, a'udhu bika min sharri ma sana'tu, abu'u laka bi ni'matika 'alayya, wa abu'u bi dhanbi, faghfir li, fa innahu la yaghfiru adh-dhunuba illa anta."* (The Sayyidul-Istighfar — the master supplication for forgiveness. Sahih al-Bukhari 6306.)
2. *"Allahumma inni as'aluka hubbaka wa hubba man yuhibbuka, wa hubba 'amalin yuqarribuni ila hubbika."* (A dua that asks for the love of Allah and the love of those who love Him — for Mahabbah.)
3. *"Allahumma la ta'zibni bi 'adhabika, wa la tuhlikni bi ghadabika, wa 'afini qabla dhalik."* (For Raja.)

### 3.3 Daily action

- Recite **Sayyidul-Istighfar** every morning. It is a *covenant* — if you die that day, you are among the people of Jannah.
- Make **one specific Tawbah** every night: name the sin, feel the regret, and resolve not to return. Do not generalise ("forgive my sins") — be specific ("I wasted time on X; I will not do it tomorrow").
- Perform **two rak'ahs of Tawbah** whenever a major sin occurs.

### 3.4 Reading list

- Surah At-Tahrim 66:8 (the verse of Tawbah).
- Surah Az-Zumar 39:53 (the limitlessness of Allah's mercy).
- The *Sayyidul-Istighfar* — Sahih al-Bukhari 6306.
- The hadith on the three who will be shaded by Allah's throne — Sahih al-Bukhari 660 (one of them is the one whose heart is attached to the mosques — a sign of Mahabbah).

## 4 · Signs of progress

- **Step 1 → 2:** Your Tawbah is not just words — you feel *oriented* toward Allah.
- **Step 2 → 3:** The guilt is replaced by hope. You no longer despair of mercy.
- **Step 3 → 4:** You find yourself *longing* for salah, for the Quran, for dhikr. The heart has turned warm.

## 5 · Stall signs

- **Stuck at Step 1:** You repent and then sin again. This is the *test* of Tawbah — it is not a one-time event. Add the Sayyidul-Istighfar in the morning and evening.
- **Stuck at Step 2:** You have turned but feel no warmth. Read Surah Ar-Rahman every night for 30 days.
- **Stuck at Step 3:** You hope but do not yet love. Find a righteous companion — love of Allah is contagious.

## 10 · Why Tawbah is in parentheses

Tawbah is a **prerequisite** for this pathway, not a step in the chain. The chain starts *after* Tawbah has been made, and shows the journey from the first turning to the warmth of Mahabbah.

If the user has not yet made Tawbah, the system points them to a *Tawbah card* (a special intervention) before showing this pathway.
