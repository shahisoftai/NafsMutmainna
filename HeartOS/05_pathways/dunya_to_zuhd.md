# Pathway 7 — Dunya → Zuhd

> The chain from attachment to the material world to the freedom of Zuhd and the peace of Ridha.

---

## 1 · Summary

```
   Hubb ad-Dunya  →  Zuhd  →  Ridha
   (Love of World)   (Asceticism)  (Satisfaction)
```

| Step | Attribute | Nature | One-line definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hubb ad-Dunya** | Negative | Excessive love of the material world — wealth, status, comfort. |
| 2 | **Zuhd** | Positive | Renunciation of attachment to the dunya; not its lawful pleasures, but its hold on the heart. |
| 3 | **Ridha** | Positive | Satisfaction with Allah's decree — past, present, future. |

## 2 · The journey

| Step | Attribute | Quranic anchor | Hadith anchor | Practitioner note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hubb ad-Dunya | At-Takathur 102:1–2 | Sahih al-Bukhari 2882 | The disease is *rivalry* in wealth and status. |
| 2 | Zuhd | Al-Hadid 57:20 | Sahih al-Bukhari 81 | Zuhd is not poverty; it is *freedom from attachment*. |
| 3 | Ridha | Al-Bayyinah 98:8 | Sahih Muslim 4501 | Ridha is the highest station. |

## 3 · Recommended practices

### 3.1 Dhikr (daily)

- *La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah* — 100×. (A dua for freedom from attachment.)
- *Allahumma la taj'aliddunya hammi* — daily. (For Zuhd.)
- *Alhamdulillah* — 100× (for Ridha).

### 3.2 Dua (memorise)

1. *"Allahumma la taj'aliddunya akbara hammi, wa la munta nihamati, wa la balagha ajali, wal-jannatu."* (For Zuhd — Sahih al-Bukhari 7381.)
2. *"Allahumma inni as'aluka ridhaka wal-jannah."* (For Ridha.)
3. *"Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-bukhl."* (For Hubb ad-Dunya.)

### 3.3 Daily action

- **Give sadaqah weekly** — a fixed amount, even if small. This breaks the hold of wealth.
- **Fast Mondays and Thursdays** — the Prophetic practice of voluntary fasting. (Sahih al-Bukhari 1166.)
- **Audit your attachments** — what do you check first thing in the morning? If it is your phone, your portfolio, or your status, that is a Hubb ad-Dunya sign.

### 3.4 Reading list

- Surah At-Takathur 102 (the chapter on the *rivalry* in wealth).
- Surah Al-Hadid 57:20 (the verse of Zuhd).
- Surah Al-Kahf 18:32–46 (the parable of the two gardens — a critique of dunya attachment).
- The hadith of the *seven shaded by Allah's throne* — Sahih al-Bukhari 660 (one of them is a young person who grew up in worship of Allah, and one of them is a man whose heart is attached to the mosque).

## 4 · Signs of progress

- **Step 1 → 2:** You can pass by a luxury item without feeling *pulled*.
- **Step 2 → 3:** You welcome whatever Allah gives you — abundance or scarcity.
- **Final:** You give away money, time, and attention without regret.

## 5 · Stall signs

- **Stuck at Step 1:** You cannot stop the inner comparison. Practice *one* weekly sadaqah for 6 weeks.
- **Stuck at Step 2:** You are detached in theory but still attached to your phone, your image, your status. Audit your screen time.
- **Stuck at Step 3:** You are detached but cannot reach satisfaction. Add Tahajjud.

## 10 · Important nuance

**Zuhd is not poverty.** It is not "dropping out of life." The classical scholars say:

> "Zuhd in the dunya is not to forbid yourself the lawful; it is to forbid the lawful from occupying your heart." — Ibn al-Qayyim

A wealthy Muslim who gives sadaqah and does not hoard is more Zuhd than a poor Muslim who is attached to what little they have. The Heart OS recommendation is *attitudinal*, not *behavioural* — it does not ask the user to give up lawful earnings, only to *wean* the heart from them.
