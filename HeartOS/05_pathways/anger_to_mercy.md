# Pathway 1 — Anger → Mercy

> The canonical chain for the disease of anger. From the hot, reactive state of Ghadab to the cool, generous state of Rahmah.

---

## 1 · Summary

```
   Ghadab  →  Sabr  →  Hilm  →  Rifq  →  Rahmah
   (Anger)   (Patience)  (Forbearance)  (Gentleness)  (Mercy)
```

| Step | Attribute | Nature | One-line definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ghadab** | Negative | The emotional response to perceived wrongdoing. |
| 2 | **Sabr** | Positive | Restraint of the self from haste; perseverance in obedience. |
| 3 | **Hilm** | Positive | Forbearance in the face of provocation. |
| 4 | **Rifq** | Positive | Gentleness in all dealings — speech, action, thought. |
| 5 | **Rahmah** | Positive | Active mercy and compassion toward creation. |

## 3 · Recommended practices

### 3.1 Dhikr (daily)

- *SubhanAllahi wa bihamdihi* — 100× in the morning. (For Sabr)
- *Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-ghadab* — after every Salah. (For Ghadab)
- *Allahumma ihdini li ahsani al-akhlaq* — once daily. (For Hilm)

### 3.2 Dua (memorise)

1. *"Allahumma ihdini li ahsani al-akhlaq, la yahdi li ahsaniiha illa anta, wa inni a'udhu bika min sayyi'iha."* (For Sabr and Hilm.)
2. *"Allahumma inni as'aluka al-hilm wa al-haya wa al-'afiyah fi al-dunya wa al-akhirah."* (For Hilm and Rifq.)
3. *"Allahumma aj'alni min awliya'ika al-rahamin."* (For Rahmah.)

### 3.3 Daily action

- When anger rises: **remain silent**, change posture (sit if standing, lie down if sitting), and perform **wudu**. This is the Prophetic prescription (Sahih al-Bukhari 6116).
- Once a week, perform an act of **deliberate gentleness** — speak softly to a server, give up a parking spot, write a kind note.

### 3.4 Reading list

- Surah Al-Imraan 3:133–134 (the virtues of the patient).
- Surah Ash-Shura 42:37–43 (the ethics of forgiveness).
- Surah Al-A'raf 7:199 (the command to forgive and be merciful).

## 4 · Signs of progress

You are moving along this chain if you notice:

- **Step 1 → 2:** Your initial reaction to provocation is *pause*, not *outburst*.
- **Step 2 → 3:** You can absorb an insult without visibly reacting.
- **Step 3 → 4:** Your default tone with family, colleagues, and strangers becomes softer.
- **Step 4 → 5:** You start to feel *compassion* for the person who provoked you.

## 5 · Stall signs

- **Stuck at Step 1:** The wudu technique is not enough. Consider a 24-hour "no opinions" fast once a week.
- **Stuck at Step 2:** You have patience but no gentleness. Add *Rifq* exercises (slow speech, no interruption).
- **Stuck at Step 3:** You are gentle but lack the active *mercy* of Step 4. Volunteer; give charity; visit the sick.

## 8 · Weekly rhythm (suggested)

| Week | Focus | Daily action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Diagnose Ghadab | Log the emotion. Note the trigger. |
| 2 | Practise Sabr | Apply the wudu technique 3×. |
| 3 | Cultivate Hilm | When corrected, respond with "thank you" once. |
| 4 | Add Rifq | Speak 20% slower than usual. |
| 5 | Practise Rahmah | One act of kindness to a stranger. |
| 6 | Consolidate | Reflect on the 6 weeks; journal the change. |
