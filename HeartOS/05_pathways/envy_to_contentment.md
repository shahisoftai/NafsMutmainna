# Pathway 3 — Envy → Contentment

> The chain from the corrosive comparison of Hasad to the settled satisfaction of Ridha.

---

## 1 · Summary

```
   Hasad  →  Shukr  →  Qanaah  →  Ridha
   (Envy)   (Gratitude)  (Contentment)  (Satisfaction)
```

| Step | Attribute | Nature | One-line definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hasad** | Negative | Disliking blessings given to others and wishing they were taken away. |
| 2 | **Shukr** | Positive | Recognising and acknowledging Allah's blessings. |
| 3 | **Qanaah** | Positive | Contentment with what one has. |
| 4 | **Ridha** | Positive | Satisfaction with Allah's decree — past, present, future. |

## 2 · The journey

## 3 · Recommended practices

### 3.1 Dhikr (daily)

- *Alhamdulillah* — 100×/day, ideally tied to a specific blessing.
- *La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah* — when envy arises. (For Qanaah)
- *SubhanAllahi wa bihamdihi* — when you see someone else's blessing. (For Shukr)

### 3.2 Dua (memorise)

1. *"Allahumma inni as'aluka ridhaka wal-jannah, wa a'udhu bika minal-'adhabi wan-nar."* (A Prophetic dua that asks for Ridha directly.)
2. *"Allahumma qanni'ni bi-ma razaqtani wa barik li fima a'taytani."* (For Qanaah.)
3. *"Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-hasadi wal-bukhl."* (For Hasad — recited before sleep.)

### 3.3 Daily action

- **Count five blessings** every night before bed. Write them down.
- When you notice envy, **make du'a for the person** you envy — by name. This breaks the comparison.
- Give away something you would normally keep. (Sadaqah dissolves Hasad.)

### 3.4 Reading list

- Surah Al-Falaq 113:5 (the protection from the evil of the envier).
- Surah An-Nahl 16:18 (the call to count blessings).
- Surah At-Talaq 65:7 (the verse of sufficiency).
- The *hadith of the rich man* — Sahih Muslim 1051.

## 4 · Signs of progress

- **Step 1 → 2:** You catch envy quickly and replace it with a blessing count.
- **Step 2 → 3:** You no longer compare what you have with others' possessions.
- **Step 3 → 4:** You welcome the difficult things in life as part of the decree.

## 5 · Stall signs

- **Stuck at Step 1:** Envy is so automatic that you cannot catch it. Add the *dua before sleep* for one month.
- **Stuck at Step 2:** You are grateful in theory but feel scarcity. Add weekly sadaqah.
- **Stuck at Step 3:** You are content with what you have but resent the decree. Read Surah At-Talaq 65:7 daily for 40 days.
