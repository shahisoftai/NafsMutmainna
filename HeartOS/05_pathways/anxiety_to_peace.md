# Pathway 2 — Anxiety → Peace

> The chain from the restlessness of anxiety to the inner stillness of Sakinah.

---

## 1 · Summary

```
   Weak Tawakkul  →  Tawakkul  →  Yaqeen  →  Sakinah
   (Weak Reliance)   (Reliance)   (Certainty)   (Tranquility)
```

| Step | Attribute | Nature | One-line definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Weak Tawakkul** | Negative | Anxiety rooted in distrust of Allah's plan. |
| 2 | **Tawakkul** | Positive | Reliance on Allah while taking the means. |
| 3 | **Yaqeen** | Positive | Certainty of faith — knowing Allah is in control. |
| 4 | **Sakinah** | Positive | Tranquility that descends upon the believer. |

## 3 · Recommended practices

### 3.1 Dhikr (daily)

- *Hasbiyallahu la ilaha illa huwa, 'alayhi tawakkaltu, wa Huwa Rabbul-'Arshil-'Azhim* — 7× morning and evening. (For Tawakkul)
- *La ilaha illa anta, Subhanaka inni kuntu min adh-dhalimin* — when anxiety is acute. (For Yaqeen)
- *Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-hammi wal-huzn, wa a'udhu bika minal-'ajzi wal-kasal* — daily. (For Sakinah)

### 3.2 Dua (memorise)

1. *"Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-hammi wal-huzn, wal-'ajzi wal-kasal, wal-bukhli wal-jubn, wa dhala'id-dayni wa ghalabatir-rijal."* (The comprehensive anxiety dua — Sahih al-Bukhari 6369.)
2. *"Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal-wakeel."* (For Tawakkul — repeated 70× in one sitting for severe distress.)
3. *"Allahumma rabban-nas, adh-habil-bas, ishfini anta ash-shafi, la shifa'a illa shifa'uka, shifa'an la yughadiru saqama."* (For Sakinah.)

### 3.3 Daily action

- **Entrust one major concern to Allah** *and* take the practical step. (Tawakkul + means.)
- Read **Ayat al-Kursi** (2:255) before sleep.
- Keep a **gratitude list** of 5 items at night.

### 3.4 Reading list

- Surah At-Talaq 65:1–3 (Tawakkul after divorce — a model).
- Surah Ar-Ra'd 13:28 (the verse of Sakinah).
- Surah Adh-Dhariyat 51:20–23 (the signs in the earth for the certain).
- The full *du'a of distress* — Sahih al-Bukhari 6369.

## 4 · Signs of progress

- **Step 1 → 2:** You name the worry, then act on the means, then leave the outcome.
- **Step 2 → 3:** You stop over-checking (email, news, tests) because you trust the outcome is in Allah's hands.
- **Step 3 → 4:** You experience a deep, quiet stillness during salah. Sleep is restful.

## 5 · Stall signs

- **Stuck at Step 1:** You name the worry but cannot let go. Add *hasbiyallahu* 100×/day for a week.
- **Stuck at Step 2:** You trust but cannot reach certainty. Add a daily 10-minute Quran reflection on Allah's names (Al-Wakil, Ar-Razzaq, Al-Hafiz).
- **Stuck at Step 3:** You have certainty in theory but not in your body. Add regular Tahajjud — the night prayer brings Sakinah.

## 8 · Weekly rhythm (suggested)

| Week | Focus | Daily action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Diagnose Weak Tawakkul | Identify 3 daily worries. Categorise: in my control / not. |
| 2 | Practise Tawakkul | For 1 worry, take a practical step + say *hasbiyallahu*. |
| 3 | Build Yaqeen | 10 min/day reflecting on one of Allah's names. |
| 4 | Welcome Sakinah | 10 min of silence after Fajr, with dhikr. |
| 5 | Consolidate Tawakkul | Apply to a major life decision. |
| 6 | Reflect | Journal the change. |
