For **Heart OS**, we deliberately restricted the content to the most reliable and widely accepted Sunni sources. The intention was **not comprehensiveness, but authenticity and safety**.

---

# 1. Primary Source

## The Noble Quran

Used for:

* Attributes
* Emotions
* Diseases of the heart
* Supplications
* Nafs states
* Reflections
* Growth pathways

Translations intended:

### English

* Saheeh International

### Urdu

* Mufti Taqi Usmani

---

# 2. Highest Authentic Hadith Sources (Sahihayn)

## 1. Sahih al-Bukhari

Compiler:

* Imam Muhammad ibn Ismail al-Bukhari

Used for:

* Character traits
* Patience
* Anger
* Mercy
* Gratitude
* Tawakkul
* Humility
* Love of Allah
* Repentance

* Sincerity

# 4. Curated Authentic Collections

## Riyad as-Salihin

Compiler:

* Imam Yahya al-Nawawi

Contains approximately 1900 carefully selected hadiths mostly from the Sahih collections.

Used heavily because of its emphasis on:

* Tazkiyah
* Character
* Repentance
* Patience
* Gratitude
* Humility
* Brotherhood
* Sincerity
* Love of Allah

([Sunnah][4])

---

# Sources intentionally avoided

❌ Weak (Da'if) narrations.

❌ Fabricated (Mawdu') narrations.

❌ Dream stories.

❌ Sufi anecdotes without chains.

❌ Internet quotations without references.

❌ Inspirational sayings falsely attributed to the Prophet ﷺ.

❌ Unverified WhatsApp material.

---

# In order of priority, Heart OS content should always follow:

```text
1. Quran
        ↓
2. Sahih al-Bukhari
        ↓
3. Sahih Muslim
        ↓
4. Riyad as-Salihin
        ↓
5. Authenticated narrations from Tirmidhi, Abu Dawud, Nasa'i and Ibn Majah
        ↓
6. Muwatta Imam Malik
        ↓
7. Hisn al-Muslim
        ↓
8. Ibn al-Qayyim
        ↓
9. Ibn Taymiyyah
        ↓
10. Ibn Rajab
        ↓
11. Al-Ghazali (with caution)
```

This hierarchy provides a strong scholarly foundation for Heart OS while keeping the content focused on **authenticity, tazkiyah (purification of the heart), and practical transformation**.
