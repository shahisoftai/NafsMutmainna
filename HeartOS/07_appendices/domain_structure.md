# Appendix C — The 10 Domains

> The 10 macro-domains of the heart. See `../01_core_tables/domains.md` for the prose.

| ID | Domain (EN) | Arabic | Description |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Iman** | الإيمان | Faith itself — its presence, growth, and protection. |
| 2 | **Tawheed & Yaqeen** | التوحيد واليقين | The oneness of Allah and the certainty of faith. |
| 3 | **Worship** | العبادة | The five pillars and the voluntary acts. |
| 4 | **Dhikr & Quran** | الذكر والقرآن | Remembrance of Allah and recitation of the Quran. |
| 5 | **Character** | الأخلاق | Akhlaq — patience, honesty, humility, generosity. |
| 6 | **Relationships** | العلاقات | How we treat family, neighbours, the wronged. |
| 7 | **Dunya** | الدنيا | Attitude toward wealth, status, time, body. |
| 8 | **Sabr & Tawakkul** | الصبر والتوكل | Patience in trials and reliance on Allah. |
| 9 | **Diseases of Heart** | أمراض القلب | Classical *amradh al-qalb*. |
| 10 | **Love of Allah & Akhirah** | محبة الله والآخرة | Mahabbah — love, longing, hope, fear. |

## C.1 · The 5 narrative macro-domains (from the docx)

The narrative `NafsMutmainna-200-Attributes.docx` groups the 200 attributes into 5 macro-domains. These are *coarser* than the 10 above and are used for narrative purposes only.

| # | Macro-domain | Theme | Approx. attribute count |
|---:|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Core Spiritual Vices & Remedies | Riya, Kibr, Ujb, Nifaq, … | ~60 |
| 2 | Emotional Vulnerabilities & Impulse Triggers | Ghadab, Khawf, Hasad, Huzn, … | ~40 |
| 3 | Social Dynamics & Interpersonal Vices | Gheebah, Namimah, Uqd, … | ~35 |
| 4 | Cognitive Traps & Mental Patterns | Waswasa, Dhann, Ghaflah, … | ~30 |
| 5 | Behavioral Habits & Self-Regulation | Sabr, Shukr, Qanaah, Ikhlas, … | ~35 |

These 5 macro-domains are a *narrative* grouping. The 10 domains in the runtime `domains` table are the canonical split.
