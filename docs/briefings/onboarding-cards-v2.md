# Onboarding Cards v2 — Nafs Mutmainna

> **Concept shift**: First 2 cards introduce the spiritual journey (Nafs-e-Ammara → Nafs-e-Mutmainna).
> Remaining 6 cards explain the app features that support this journey.

---

## Card 1 — The Hidden Battle

**Headline:** The Battle Within

**Body:**
Every day, a war rages inside you — between your soul seeking peace and whispers pulling you toward darkness.

Pride. Envy. Anger. Greed. Laziness.

These negative traits aren't just flaws. They are the fingerprints of **Nafs-e-Ammara** — the soul commanded by desire — and the gateway through which Shaytan influences you.

The good news? You have the power to change.

**Icon:** `⚔️` (crossed swords silhouette or heart-shield)

**Gradient:** Deep indigo → dark purple (`1A1A2E` → `16213E` → `0F3460`)

---

## Card 2 — Your Destination

**Headline:** Nafs Mutmainna Awaits

**Body:**
> **"O soul in complete rest and satisfaction!
> Return to your Lord, well-pleased and well-pleasing!
> Enter among My servants!
> Enter My Paradise!"**
>
> — Surah Al-Fajr (89:27-30)

This is your destination. A soul at peace. A soul beloved to Allah. A soul worthy of Paradise.

This app is your companion on that journey — helping you identify the negative traits, understand their source, and transform your heart toward tranquility.

**Icon:** `🕌` (dome silhouette or paradise gates)

**Gradient:** Emerald green → gold (`0D3D12` → `1B5E20` → `D4AF37`)

---

## Card 3 — Daily Check-In

**Headline:** Check In With Your Heart

**Body:**
A 60-second daily check-in — the heart of your transformation.

1. **Pick** the emotion you feel right now
2. **Adjust** how strongly you feel it
3. **Note** what's behind it (optional)

This simple act builds self-awareness — the first step toward changing what you notice inside yourself.

**Icon:** `📝` (edit/pencil icon)

**Gradient:** Forest green → bright green (`1B5E20` → `388E3C` → `66BB6A`)

---

## Card 4 — Heart Analysis

**Headline:** See What Your Heart Reveals

**Body:**
After every check-in, HeartOS analyses **3–5 heart attributes** active in you right now.

You also see your current growth path through the four stations of the Nafs.

The more you check in, the more accurate the picture becomes — and the clearer your path forward.

**Icon:** `🔍` (insights/magnifying glass)

**Gradient:** Teal → aqua (`004D40` → `00695C` → `26A69A`)

---

## Card 5 — Your Nafs Meter

**Headline:** Track Your Soul's Progress

**Body:**
The four stations of the soul in Islam:

**Ammarah** → **Lawwamah** → **Mulhamah** → **Mutmainnah**

Your daily check-in moves the meter. Watch the trend — a quiet nudge toward peace, or a warning when you drift.

Think of it as a fitness tracker, but for your heart.

**Icon:** `📊` (speed/gauge icon)

**Gradient:** Red → amber → green (the Nafs spectrum) (`E53935` → `FB8C00` → `43A047`)

---

## Card 6 — Personal Prescription

**Headline:** A Prescription Just for You

**Body:**
Based on your detected attributes, receive a daily prescription — each card is one of six types:

📖 **Quran** · ✋ **Hadith** · 🤲 **Dua**
💎 **Allah Names** · 🔁 **Dhikr** · ✅ **Action**

Mark each as **Done** or **Skip** to refine tomorrow's prescription.

**Icon:** `💊` (medical/prescription icon)

**Gradient:** Gold → amber (`FF8F00` → `FFB300` → `FFCA28`)

---

## Card 7 — Your 15-Day Journey

**Headline:** Watch Your Transformation Unfold

**Body:**
Every check-in is saved to your private journey.

- See a **trend** of your last 15 days
- Discover which **Allah Names** are guiding you most
- Review **day-by-day** what surfaced

Tap any day to dive into the full picture. Watch yourself grow.

**Icon:** `📅` (calendar/timeline icon)

**Gradient:** Blue → sky blue (`1565C0` → `1976D2` → `42A5F5`)

---

## Card 8 — The 8 Master Pathways

**Headline:** Every Struggle Has a Map

**Body:**
All spiritual struggles follow one of **8 mapped pathways** — from a challenging emotion to the soul at peace.

Example: **Anger → Patience → Gentleness → Mercy → Compassion**

HeartOS shows your current path and where it leads. You're never lost — the map exists.

**Icon:** `🗺️` (account tree/pathway icon)

**Gradient:** Purple → violet (`4A148C` → `6A1B9A` → `8E24AA`)
