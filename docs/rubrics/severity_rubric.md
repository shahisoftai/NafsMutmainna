# Severity Rubric — HeartOS Emotions

> **Implements RI-4.1** of `docs/scholar-audit-remediation-plan.md`.

Each of the 50 core emotions carries a `Severity_Weight` (1–10). This rubric
documents how the weight is determined. Severity is **editorial**, not
scriptural — there is no single verse or hadith that ranks the diseases of
the heart in order. The rubric derives from three classical criteria:

1. **Proximity to kufr or shirk** — the closer a marad al-qalb is to negation
   of tawhid, the higher its severity. (Ghazali, Ihya' 21.)
2. **Reversibility** — how difficult the cure is once the disease is
   established. (Ibn al-Qayyim, Ighasat al-Lahfan.)
3. **Impact on the fard acts of worship** — how much the marad obstructs the
   user's daily salah, fasting, zakat, hajj, and dhikr. (al-Muhasibi,
   al-Ri'aya.)

## Tier 9–10 (Most severe)

The disease is at the root of the spiritual state. It either negates tawhid,
introduces shirk, or constitutes the destruction of the amal (deeds).

|---|---|---|
| 21 | Hasad | Wishes removal of Allah's blessings from others — root of all *amradh* |
| 2  | Jealousy (Hasad) | Same root as above |
| 13 | Showing Off (Riya) | Shirk al-asghar; nullifies deeds (Quran 107:4-6) |
| 4  | Hypocrisy (Nifaq) | Difference between outward/inward is at the root of iman |
| 15 | Love of Dunya (excessive) | Quran 9:24 — competes with love of Allah |
| 1  | Arrogance (Kibr) | First sin of Iblis; Ibn al-Qayyim's first station |

| 15 | Love of Dunya | Quran 102 — at-Takathur, the destructive rivalry |
| 60 | Despair of mercy (Qanut) | Opposite of raja; tantamount to denying Allah's names |
